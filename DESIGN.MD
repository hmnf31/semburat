## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a refined, thoughtful editorial aesthetic tailored for publishing, long-form content, and knowledge dissemination. Rooted in warmth, serenity, and intellectual clarity, it trades cold corporate tech minimalism for tactile, literary poise.

### Brand Personality

- **Quietly Authoritative:** Informed, measured, and trustworthy without posturing.
- **Warm & Organic:** Earth-leaning tones, natural textures, and open breathing room evoke the quiet sanctuary of a modern reading room or gallery catalog.
- **Meticulous & Simple:** Strict alignment, subtle hairline boundaries, and typographic precision eliminate cognitive strain.

### Target Audience & Emotional Impact

Designed for discerning readers, cultural thinkers, creators, and professionals who value clarity over noise. The interface cultivates visual calm, inviting prolonged engagement, focused reading, and contemplative exploration without sensory fatigue.

## Colors

The color architecture is built around warm, earthy organic tones set against an off-white cream foundation, rejecting stark `#FFFFFF` canvases in favor of gentle light reflection.

### Application Roles

- **Primary (`#C25E43` - Terracotta / Semburat):** Used selectively for key calls-to-action, active highlights, reading progress anchors, and critical accents. It provides warmth and cultural resonance without dominating the screen.
- **Secondary (`#2D4A3E` - Forest Subur):** A grounding, deep botanical green used for editorial tags, verified statuses, category accents, and high-trust anchors.
- **Tertiary (`#475569` - Slate):** Cool mineral slate applied to balanced iconography, technical meta tags, and structured secondary elements.
- **Neutral (`#FAFAF7` - Warm Parchment):** The global default viewport surface. Softened with a hint of warm stone to eliminate screen glare.
- **Surface Roles:**
  - `surface-card` (`#FFFFFF`): Elevated cards and reading canvases, establishing delicate contrast against the `#FAFAF7` background.
  - `surface-cream` (`#F5F4EE`): Embedded callouts, subtle pill backdrops, table headers, and secondary wells.
- **Text & Borders:**
  - `text-ink` (`#1C1917`): Deep stone black, softer than pure `#000000` for effortless legibility.
  - `text-muted` (`#78716C`): Warm secondary text for metadata, dates, and captions.
  - `border-hairline` (`#E7E5DF`): Whispering borders replacing standard drop shadows.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy harmonizes the literary presence of **Newsreader** (display and section titles) with the crisp, human geometry of **Plus Jakarta Sans** (body text, UI elements, and navigation).

### Editorial Balance

- **Serif Warmth (`Newsreader`):** Applied exclusively to editorial headlines, article titles, pull quotes, and hero statements. It imparts craft, nuance, and pacing to the reading experience.
- **Contemporary Clarity (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Employed for long-form prose and functional UI controls. Its open counters and balanced stroke weight guarantee effortless readability on high-density displays.
- **Rhythm & Line Heights:** Body copy favors generous vertical metrics (`line-height: 1.625` to `1.7`) to give eyes resting space between lines.
- **Case & Tracking:** Small meta labels utilize elevated letter-spacing (`0.06em`) and uppercase styling for precise visual anchoring.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is anchored on an editorial grid designed to preserve wide, uninterrupted white space and disciplined vertical rhythm.

### Grid Framework

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid system capped at a maximum reading width of `1200px` for discovery feeds and `720px` for focused article prose. Gutters are fixed at `1.5rem` (`24px`) with outer canvas margins of `3rem` (`48px`).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.25rem` gutters and `2rem` margins.
- **Mobile (&lt;768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` (`20px`) margins, maximizing edge-to-edge readability without feeling crowded.

### Spacing Philosophy

Layout decisions favor intentional macro-whitespace (using `space-xl` and generous section paddings of `4rem` to `6rem`) to let high-value content breathe. Interior component padding maintains compact restraint, keeping related elements cleanly bounded.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system rejects heavy blurs, stacked elevation drops, and dramatic box shadows. Depth is communicated strictly through surface layering and hairline separation.

### Depth Mechanisms

1. **Low-Contrast Outlines ("Hairline Borders"):** Containers, cards, and input fields rely on crisp `1px` borders in `border-hairline` (`#E7E5DF`).
2. **Subtle Surface Contrasts:** Interactive cards set their surface to pure `#FFFFFF` against the ambient canvas of `#FAFAF7`, providing immediate visual separation without shadow artifacts.
3. **Ambient Micro-Lift (Hover Only):** For interactive cards, a faint, warm ambient shadow (`0 4px 20px -2px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.04)`) accompanies a delicate `border-subtle` shift, signaling responsiveness with absolute discretion.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary uses restrained, soft geometric curves (`roundedness: 1`), honoring paper, bookbinding, and printed journals rather than pill-heavy mobile apps.

- **Cards, Modals & Panels:** Built with `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / `8px`) corners, giving a clean and structured edge.
- **Inputs & Standard Buttons:** Use `rounded-md` (`0.375rem` / `6px`) for tactile control surfaces.
- **Pills & Status Chips:** Use fully rounded geometry (`rounded-full`) sparingly for micro-badges and tags, contrasting nicely against the square-leaning cards.

## Components

### Buttons

- **Primary:** Background in primary terracotta (`#C25E43`), text `#FFFFFF`, subtle inset transition on press. Rounded to `0.375rem`. Zero shadow.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background transparent, border `1px solid #E7E5DF`, text `text-ink` (`#1C1917`). On hover, background shifts to `surface-cream` (`#F5F4EE`) with border darkening to `#D6D3CB`.
- **Ghost:** Borderless, text `text-ink`, transitions to `surface-cream` on hover.

### Cards

- **Editorial Cards:** Rendered on `#FFFFFF` with a `1px solid #E7E5DF` hairline border. Interior padding set to `1.5rem` (`space-lg`). Cards feature a structured vertical flow: category label (`label-sm`), serif headline (`headline-sm` / `Newsreader`), brief excerpt (`body-sm`), and author/meta baseline.
- **Interactive State:** Hover effects subtly deepen the border to `#D6D3CB` with a micro-elevation offset of `-1px`.

### Input Fields

- Built on `#FFFFFF` with `1px solid #E7E5DF` borders and `0.375rem` radius. Placeholder text is tinted in `text-muted` (`#78716C`).
- **Focus State:** Transitions to `1px solid #C25E43` with an ultra-soft terracotta glow ring (`ring-1 ring-[#C25E43]/20`).

### Chips & Meta Badges

- Compact tags styled with `rounded-full`, padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`, font `label-sm`.
- **Forest Variant:** Background `#2D4A3E/10`, text `#2D4A3E`.
- **Terracotta Variant:** Background `#C25E43/10`, text `#C25E43`.
- **Neutral Variant:** Background `surface-cream`, text `text-ink`, border `1px solid #E7E5DF`.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)

- Distinct `18px` boundary in `#D6D3CB`.
- When checked, fills with terracotta (`#C25E43`) displaying a clean white geometric check/dot indicator.

### Editorial Dividers & Callouts

- Horizontal separators are strictly `1px` lines tinted with `border-hairline`.
- Callout quotes feature a left accent border in terracotta (`2px solid #C25E43`) on a warm `surface-cream` background with indented serif italic typography.
